# Multivariable Regression and Valuation Model

Day 81 of Udemy's *100 Days of Code*: you are an analyst at a real estate development company in Boston in the 1970s and build a model that values a house from its characteristics (rooms, distance to employment centres, poverty of the area, students per teacher and more). This wiki explains what each step teaches. Every step has a picture generated from the code.

![The seven steps from data to a house valuation](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/081-multivariable-regression-and-valuation-model/raw/branch/main/docs/images/step-0-overview.png)

| Page | What you gain |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](Getting-Started) | A `.venv`, the install, and how to run, test and check the project on Linux and Windows |
| [Step 1: Goals and data exploration](Step-1-Goals-And-Data) | What the task is, what the Boston data holds, whether it is clean, and what the descriptive statistics say |
| [Step 2: Feature distributions](Step-2-Distributions) | How are prices, commutes, rooms and highway access distributed, and how many homes touch the river? |
| [Step 3: Relationships in the data](Step-3-Relationships) | Which features move together, and what do you expect before you model? |

The question of the assignment: can the characteristics of a house predict its price, how good is the model on houses it has not seen, and does a log transformation of the price make it better?
